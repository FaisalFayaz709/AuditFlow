# Forbidden Deviations

The following deviations are not allowed in the active AuditFlow implementation:

- Certification, legal-compliance guarantee, automatic-compliance, or auditor-replacement claims.
- Browser JWT access-token authentication for the MVP browser app.
- Microservices, Kubernetes, event-streaming-first architecture, or multiple databases per module for the initial implementation.
- Public permanent evidence URLs or direct exposure of storage keys.
- AI approval of evidence, mappings, applicability, readiness, or legal/compliance conclusions.
- Readiness calculations based on AI confidence, unapproved evidence, unapproved mappings, expired evidence, superseded evidence, archived evidence, or informational controls.
- Task completion being treated as evidence or mapping approval.
- Cross-tenant existence leakage.
