# Incident Notification Assessment

Status: BLOCKED until incident process is assigned.

## Incident classes requiring assessment

- suspected cross-tenant access
- evidence object exposure
- credential leak
- malware upload or malware download exposure
- unauthorized auditor access
- AI provider data-handling incident
- backup/restore integrity failure

## Assessment record

| Incident ID | Date | Tenants affected | Evidence affected | Notification required | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|

## Required immediate actions

1. Disable affected route or feature where necessary.
2. Preserve logs and audit events.
3. Rotate relevant credentials.
4. Identify affected tenants and data categories.
5. Assess notification obligations under customer contracts/policies.
6. Document final incident report.
